Lithium (Li)

Rechargeable Batteries

warning Harmful Effects

If improperly disposed of, lithium-ion batteries can cause landfill fires. As they degrade, toxic chemicals can leach into water supplies, harming aquatic life and polluting drinking water sources.

eco Environmental Benefits

Proper recycling recovers valuable metals like cobalt and nickel, reducing the need for destructive new mining operations. It ensures safe processing that prevents toxic leakage and fires.
